 TinyMIME.h
 Splits a received message into its parts. Every part is delivered to a visitor with its data as a stream. The message is read as a stream, see TinyMIMEReader.h.
 
 TinyMIMEReader.h
 Reads a received message as a stream: the parts of the mail come one after the other, and their data is decoded while it is read. The message is never held in RAM; only a line or two and the headers of the part are.

 Header-only SMTP client for Gmail with an Arduino style API.
